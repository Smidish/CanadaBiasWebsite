About the numbers on this site

This is a working prototype of the presentation, not a publication of results. Every figure, quotation, outlet name, party name and account handle here is a placeholder, generated by a seeded random process so the interface can be built and tested. Nothing on this site describes any real news organisation, political party, candidate or person, and no number here should be cited.

The measurement definitions, the coding schemes and the stated limits are the real ones. When the analysis is complete, the data behind each figure is swapped in and the pages stay as they are.
